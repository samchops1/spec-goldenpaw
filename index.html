How the day runs

Cancellation

Cancellation must be made 24 hours before your set appointment or a $25 inconvenience fee will be added to your next visit.

Arrival times are subject to change

Your groomer can face challenges on the road that are beyond our control and cannot always make the time requested. Appointment times are estimated. If the groomer is not able to make your requested time they will advise via the phone number on file for your account.
